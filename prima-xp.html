+ / −

All interconnections are + or −. Arithmetic coupling. No event bus, no DI, no hidden state.

Axiom I: Synthesis with richness — Every decision holds thesis and antithesis. T + A = A + T. The gap (richness) signals when to investigate, not merge.

Axiom II: Cross-domain simplification — True simplification widens. A rendering bug is never just a rendering bug. The root cause lives in a different room.

Axiom III: Arithmetic architecture — Two kinds of thing: joint (as) and computation (not_as). Joints carry no value. Computations carry no position. All connections are + or −.
