Three primitives
as
Positive constraint
A value counts as connected. The gear meshes, turns blue, and drives its neighbors through the link.
not_as
Negative constraint
A value absolutely does not connect. The gear is orange, still, excluded from the turning frame.
fn[n]
Categorical computation
Work selected by index from a function space. Gold — the axle where structure meets motion.
Not a framework. Not a syntax. A turn passed through a break — and everything else is the silence between teeth.
