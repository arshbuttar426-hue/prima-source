as

The joint. Pure position. Carries no value.

The as is not a thing — it is the space between things. It does not compute, does not store, does not decide. It simply says: this function adds, that one subtracts. The entire architecture is the arrangement of as nodes. Change the as chain, change the question the system answers.

Every framework introduces a new kind of entity. This introduces exactly one: the joint. No events, no messages, no dependency injection — just + and −.

as has no value. as is the value of position.
